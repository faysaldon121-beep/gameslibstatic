Overview

Story

In the sprawling city of Los Santos, Grand Theft Auto V unfolds the complex stories of three protagonists: Michael, Trevor, and Franklin. The game begins with a flashback to a daring bank heist that goes terribly awry. Michael and Trevor, along with a third accomplice, find themselves in a frantic escape from the law. Amidst the chaos, Trevor is forced into a desperate decision as he watches his closest friends fall. This moment defines their paths forward: Michael fakes his death and enters witness protection, living a quiet suburban life, while Trevor disappears into the deserts outside Los Santos, haunted by the ghosts of his past. GTA V masterfully navigates the lives of these characters across the sprawling, open world of Los Santos. From high-stakes heists to deep personal quests, players are drawn into the narratives that bind these characters together. This is a story of survival, friendship, and the harsh realities of loyalty and betrayal in the world of organized crime.
