Call of Duty: Black Ops III

Action · PC

Overview

Story

Call of Duty: Black Ops III is set in 2065, in a dark, dystopian future where a new breed of Black Ops soldier emerges. The lines are blurred between humanity and the cutting-edge military robotics that define the future of combat. Players follow a team of cybernetically enhanced super-soldiers as they navigate a world plunged into chaos by climate change and technological advancements.

Gameplay

The game introduces a new momentum-based, chained-movement system, allowing players to fluidly move through environments using controlled thrust jumps, slides, and mantling abilities. The campaign is designed for four-player cooperative gameplay. Multiplayer features a new 'Specialist' system, which lets players choose from nine elite soldiers, each with their own unique look, personality, and a special weapon or ability. The Zombies mode, 'Shadows of Evil', offers a separate narrative set in a 1940s film-noir-inspired world.
