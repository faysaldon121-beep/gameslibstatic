Story

Need for Speed Hot Pursuit Remastered revives the classic game with enhanced graphics and more immersive driving experiences. The game delivers high-stakes chases between cops and racers, where players can either play as law enforcement and hunt down offenders or as racers who push their speeds to evade capture. Gameplay

Hot Pursuit Remastered focuses on high-speed chases with special abilities for both cops and racers. Players can use tools like roadblocks, EMPs, and deploy spike strips to disable opponents. The game also includes varied race and pursuit locations across multiple environments. With a comprehensive progression system, players unlock new cars and upgrade their skills, alongside single-player and competitive online modes.
