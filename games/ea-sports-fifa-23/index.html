Overview

Story

"EA SPORTS FIFA 23" delivers an immersive football experience that makes you feel like you're on the pitch. While the game doesn't follow a traditional story, it offers multiple modes that let you create your own narrative. Whether you're building your dream team in "Ultimate Team" or pursuing a professional career in "Career Mode," there's something for everyone. This edition also introduces the FIFA World Cup Qatar 2022 and FIFA Women's World Cup Australia and New Zealand 2023, allowing players to compete in the biggest tournaments.

Gameplay

"FIFA 23" introduces major gameplay improvements, including the new HyperMotion2 system, which enhances player movement with real-match data for more realistic animations. The game supports more accurate passing, improved shooting mechanics, and smarter AI for a smooth and engaging experience. You can play across various modes, including "Ultimate Team," where you build your dream squad with current players and football legends, or challenge friends in local or online matches.
