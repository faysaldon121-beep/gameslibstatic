Overview

Story

Need for Speed: Undercover follows a player who takes on the role of an undercover driver working for the police to infiltrate a dangerous crime syndicate. Tasked with completing risky races and missions, the player must earn the trust of gang leaders to uncover their criminal operations. The story is filled with twists and turns, blending high-speed racing with intense chase sequences.

Gameplay

The gameplay in Need for Speed: Undercover focuses on high-speed, action-packed racing, combining open-world street races with thrilling police chases. Players can explore a vast open world, including cities and highways, while completing missions and races. Additionally, players can fully customize their cars, enhancing both performance and appearance to dominate the competition.
