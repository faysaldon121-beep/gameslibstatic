Story

Verdun is a multiplayer first-person shooter set during World War I, focusing on the brutal trench warfare of the Western Front. Players can choose to fight for either the French or German forces, experiencing the harrowing realities of war through various historical battles and locations.

The gameplay in Verdun emphasizes teamwork and strategy, with players engaging in intense firefights across authentic battlefields. Players can choose from different classes, each with unique abilities and weapons, and must work together to capture objectives and defeat the enemy team.
