Overview

Story

Ghost of Tsushima is set on the Japanese island of Tsushima during the Mongol invasion in the 13th century. Players take on the role of Jin Sakai, a young samurai who must protect his homeland from the invading forces. After a crushing defeat, Jin is forced to confront the dilemma of upholding his samurai code of honor or adopting new and unconventional tactics to defeat the enemy. The story explores themes of honor, sacrifice, and internal conflict as Jin fights to liberate his country.

Gameplay

The gameplay in Ghost of Tsushima blends traditional sword combat with stealth mechanics. Players can engage in precise, skill-based sword duels, parrying and striking at the right moments, or they can opt for a stealthy approach, utilizing surprise attacks and evasion. The open-world environment allows for free exploration of vast landscapes filled with side quests, battles, and secrets to uncover, offering players a rich and immersive experience.
