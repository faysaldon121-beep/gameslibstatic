Overview

Story

ELDEN RING is set in a mythical world known as the 'Lands Between,' following the destruction of the Elden Ring and the disappearance of Queen Marika the Eternal. Players take on the role of a 'Tarnished,' an exiled warrior who must collect the shattered pieces of the Elden Ring to become the Elden Lord and rule the Lands Between. The story is steeped in mystery, with deep lore crafted by George R. R. Martin in collaboration with FromSoftware, unfolding through the game’s vast environments and its enigmatic characters.

Gameplay

ELDEN RING offers a gameplay experience that combines tough, tactical combat with the freedom of exploration in a massive open world. Players can wield a variety of weapons, magic, and special abilities to face formidable foes and challenging bosses. The game features diverse environments that can be freely explored on horseback or on foot, with hidden secrets, side quests, and challenges adding to the immersive experience. Character development and weapon upgrades are crucial as players face increasingly difficult threats.
